## How the keys are built
Every key = `natural_pose.json` merged with layered deltas (bones the stance does not list come from the stance or get a
local_delta of their own; nothing falls back to the rest pose). Each layer is zero at t=0 and t=6.0, so k00 IS the
natural stance (max difference 0.0 deg) and the seam is exact. Layers and lags (overlap):
- Breath, 2 per loop (3.0 s each, inhale peaks 1.5 s / 4.5 s): hips rx, spine_01-03 lift (-0.8/-3/-1.8 deg), neck/head
  compensate +0.45 s late, shoulders shrug 4-4.5 deg +0.15 s late, both arms +0.3 s late (shield forearm rises 5 deg,
  hand_l, fingers_l +0.45 s).
- Sway over the RIGHT leg 2.3-5.6 s (full 3.6-4.3): pelvis 4.5 cm toward his right, hips ry +6.5 rz -2, legs re-angled so
  the feet stay planted (ak solve: foot heads move <= 0.4 cm, both feet on the floor within 1.6 mm on every key);
  spine counters 0.2 s late, shoulders tilt 0.2 s late, arms follow 0.35-0.5 s late.
- Head looks to his left 3.5-5.6 s (head rz 10, neck rz 4, small tilt), chest follows 0.3 s late; small nod 5.2-5.95 s.
- Sword hand: slow pendulum (one cycle + a third harmonic) cascading shoulder -> forearm -> wrist (0.2/0.35/0.5 s lags),
  grip flex at 1.4-2.1 s and 5.0-5.6 s (wrist + fingers_r).
