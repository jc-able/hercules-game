- **walk** | video window 1.000-3.167 s (one cycle, `ak cycle` from t0 1.0: period 2.167 s = 52 frames @24, IoU 0.956/0.978; calib_iou 0.94/0.96) | loop, length 2.1667 s, fps 24 | 27 keys (every 2 frames + frame 15) | events: none (SPEC lists none for walk) | IoU (ak pose, front/side) per 4-frame key: f00 .904/.841, f04 .898/.807, f08 .902/.806, f12 .908/.841, f16 .895/.858, f20 .902/.879, f24 .908/.837, f28 .896/.894, f32 .904/.890, f36 .879/.881, f40 .872/.874, f44 .894/.858, f48 .910/.877; mean 0.877 (front 0.898, side 0.857), strip mean 0.875 over 33 samples; leg band (below 3.6 m, side) 0.73 vs 0.57 for the standing rest pose | floor contact every frame -0.009..+0.017 m.
- How it was made: the poses are authored by `poses_walk/gen_walk.py` (params `poses_walk/gen_params.json`), a side-plane 2-bone IK walk on the real rig in aim form (absolute space): lateral-sequence gait, footfalls read off the video (left fore swing centred at 1.33 s, right fore 2.15 s, right hind 1.65 s, left hind ~2.8 s; side checked in the front panel: the screen-right = character-left forefoot lifts at 1.25-1.4 s), duty 0.8, fore stride 3.0 m, hind 3.4 m, body 0.1 m lower than rest, heel lift about the toe when a planted foot would be out of reach (max 15-20 deg), swing reach forward on the forelegs. Gait parameters were tuned numerically against the 13 frames (no image reads per try): stance width, stride, swing reach, body height, neck and tail amplitudes. Dense keys because the poses come from IK: with 4-frame keys the bezier in-betweens pushed a toe 0.12-0.18 m through the floor at touch-down; every 2 frames + frame 15 is clean.
- Secondary motion (authored, not traced): all nine necks bob twice per cycle (1.0 deg per link, alternating phase offsets per neck so they never move together, centre neck 0.7x) with a slight side sway (0.4 deg/link); tail swings once per cycle (4 deg/link, travelling wave, tip to the character's left at ~0.25 s clip time as in the video); spine yaw sway 0.5 deg/link; 5 cm body bob.
- What does not match and why: (1) Seedance stretches the legs: in the video the forelegs reach ~1 m further forward than the rig's straight leg can (front leg is fully straight at rest), so the lifted foreleg reaches at mid-swing and lands nearer; a version that copied the reach scored side 0.845 but left planted feet floating up to 0.9 m, so it was dropped. (2) The video's neck motion is irregular; larger neck sway (1.5 deg/link) cost 0.05 front IoU, so neck motion is kept small. (3) The video gait is not perfectly periodic (cycle 2.17 s from t0 1 s, 1.875 s from t0 4 s); one 2.17 s cycle is used. (4) Front-foot heel lift up to 20 deg at touch-down (out-of-reach forward target) - reads as landing on the toes; reduce `front_scale` in gen_params.json if it bothers in game.
