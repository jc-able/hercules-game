# hydra / hurt (reference video "hurt")

hurt | window t0-t1 = 1.25-2.875 s (first take; second take 4.5-6.5 s is the same move) | 8 keys at clip t 0, 0.125, 0.375, 0.583, 0.792, 1.0, 1.25, 1.625 (k0 k1 k2 k3 k3b k4 k5 k0) | key IoU front/side mean: k0 0.925, k1 0.801, k2 0.766, k3 0.737, k3b 0.739, k4 0.749, k5 0.765, end k0 0.846 | length 1.633 s (30 fps), loop false | events: none (SPEC names no hurt event) | strip IoU mean 0.775 (25 samples), feet on the floor all through (lowest_z 0).

- Starts and ends on the rest/sheet pose (k0 = video frame at 1.25 s, IoU 0.92): the same pose the other hydra clips start from, so it blends into idle.
- Traced: the centre neck lean back + head snapped straight up, the eight side necks fanning out and back (fan angle, back lean, curl and head angle per rank inner->outer), tail dropping, and the snap back with the centre neck overshooting forward (k5, 2.5 s).
- Authored: per-neck phase offsets (+-2-4 deg on k1/k2/k4, +-6-9 deg thrash on k3/k3b, centre neck +-2 deg sideways) so the eight heads do not move as one; the video's heads thrash too but nine heads cannot be traced one by one.
- Does not match: (1) the video's body drops ~0.4-0.5 m in the flinch (a framing fit wanted view_offset z -0.48 at 1.375 s while 1.25 s needs none); not keyed, the body stays level (a crouch needs leg IK; a whole-body pitch lifted the forefeet). (2) Seedance draws the tail shorter and lying lower than the rig's long tail: blue/red band at the tail in every side frame, rest pose included. (3) At 2.417 s the video necks are already folded back while ours are still half open (recovery is ~0.1 s later). (4) The video's side heads at 2.5 s sit lower and more curled than the rig's rest heads (front IoU 0.73 on k5).
- Method: poses are generated by work/anim/hydra_hurt/opt.py (aim-form side necks + chain bends) and tuned by a coordinate search scored by `ak pose` IoU; `ak fit` cannot work on aim/chain poses.
